Privacy

How Noise to Signal handles personal data. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Who we are

Noise to Signal (noise-to-signal.fyi) is operated by Kjeld Schmidt (sole operator; EU). For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email kjeld.schmidt@the-coding-kind.com.

Under the GDPR, Kjeld Schmidt is the controller of personal data processed through this service.

What this service does

Noise to Signal is a personal news product. It aggregates feeds you choose or enable, ranks stories for your brief, and personalizes what you see using settings you configure and preference memory learned from your feedback and taste answers.

Data we process

  • Account identity. When you sign in with Google, we receive identifiers Google shares for authentication (typically name, email address, and a stable account id). We do not store end-user passwords.
  • Product settings. Content languages, locations of interest, muted or custom feeds, and similar configuration needed to run your account.
  • Relevance and taste signals. Feedback on stories (relevant / not / skip) and answers to short taste questions.
  • Preference memory. From those signals we extract and store lasting preference records (for example topics, tones, or sources you tend to want or avoid) so returning briefs stay personalized. These records are stored in Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Memory in an EU AWS region, scoped to your user id, and used only to personalize your own experience.
  • News chat. Chat turns for the in-product assistant are kept in your browser for that session/device and are not stored as a server-side conversation history. Messages you send may be processed in the EU by our application and Amazon Bedrock to generate a reply. Preference-relevant signals from product use may still update preference memory as described above.
  • Technical data. Session cookies required to keep you signed in, and ordinary server logs (for example timestamps, request paths, approximate IP) needed to operate, secure, and debug the service.

Why we process it (legal bases)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Creating your account, delivering the brief and stories, applying your settings, and maintaining preference memory so the product works as offered.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Securing the service, preventing abuse, and improving reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent only where required for optional features that are not strictly needed to provide the core product (we do not use non-essential advertising or analytics cookies today).

Where data lives and who helps us

Application data (account, settings, feedback) is stored in our databases hosted on Amazon Web Services in the EU. Preference memory is stored in Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Memory in the EU. Model inference for ranking, extraction, and chat runs on Amazon Bedrock with processing kept in the EU (in-region or EU geographic inference).

Google acts as identity provider for sign-in. AWS acts as our infrastructure and AI processing provider. We do not sell personal data. Amazon Bedrock does not use your prompts or outputs to train foundation models.

Cookies

We use a session cookie that is strictly necessary for authentication and CSRF protection. We do not set advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Retention

  • Account and settings data are kept while your account remains active.
  • Preference memory records are retained while needed to personalize your briefs, subject to retention limits we configure on AgentCore Memory, and are deleted when your account is deleted or when you ask us to erase them.
  • Operational logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA/UK, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact kjeld.schmidt@the-coding-kind.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To erase preference memory or close your account, email us from the address linked to your Google sign-in so we can verify the request.

Changes

If we change how we process personal data in a material way, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
